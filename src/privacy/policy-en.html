Gamble Hunters Demo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

ASHUR Inc. ("we", "us") processes only the personal information we truly need to provide the Gamble Hunters demo (the "Game"). This policy explains what we process and why, and how you can turn collection off or ask us to delete your data. We publish this policy under Article 30 of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act ("PIPA").

1. Data Minimization

  • The Game has no account sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or payment information.
  • The gameplay data we use to improve the Game never includes your name, email address, Steam ID, or nickname. Instead, we use a random ID generated for each installation. Because this ID can link records from the same device, we treat it as personal information.
  • We collect gameplay data only if you agree when you first launch the Game. You can turn it off in Settings at any time, which also deletes the data already collected (Section 3).
  • Your Steam ID is used only to tell participants apart in party chat (Section 2.B).
  • We do not use personal information for advertising, marketing, personalized services, or automated decision-making.

2. What We Process and Why

A. Gameplay data (game analytics)

PurposeTuning difficulty and balance, finding bugs and points where players drop off, and improving loading and multiplayer stability
DataRandom IDs (installation ID, session ID); device and environment information (platform, device model, operating system and version, CPU and GPU model, memory size, screen resolution, language, time zone, and country estimated from your IP address — the IP address is used only to estimate the country); game version; gameplay progress (run and stage results, time taken, kills and deaths, remaining debt and deadline); in-game economy records (shop purchases, weapon upgrades, debt repayments, and per-minigame casino play counts with total bets and payouts — all in in-game currency); loading and connection records; whether you play solo, as host, or as a guest, and party size
How collectedAutomatically while the Game runs, only if you agree when you first launch the Game. You can turn it off in Settings at any time (Section 3).
Legal basisFor users in Korea: Article 15(1)(6) of PIPA (our legitimate interests), and in addition we let you choose whether data is collected. For users elsewhere: your consent
RetentionIndividual records are deleted automatically 13 months after collection. Aggregate metrics derived from them (for example, daily active users) are kept until the Game's service ends or until you request deletion.

B. Party voice and text chat

PurposeProviding voice and text chat between party members in multiplayer
DataSteam ID (to tell chat participants apart), channel name containing the Steam lobby number, a random player ID issued by Unity's authentication service, device ID, truncated IP address, text chat messages, microphone audio
How collectedWhen you join a Steam lobby, you are connected to the party chat channel automatically (LAN parties use a text-only channel). Microphone audio is sent according to the microphone mode in Settings (Off, Push to Talk, or Voice Activation).
Legal basisArticle 15(1)(4) of PIPA (performance of a contract)
RetentionVoice is relayed in real time only and is never recorded or stored. Text chat is deleted after 7 days; player IDs, device IDs, and IP addresses are deleted within 30 days.

3. Consent and How to Turn Collection Off

  • The Game includes the Unity Analytics SDK, which works much like a website cookie. The SDK stores a random installation ID on your device and, if you agree, automatically collects the information in Section 2.A while the Game runs.
  • The first time you launch the Game, we ask whether you agree to gameplay data collection. We collect it only if you choose Agree; if you choose Decline, nothing is collected. Nothing is sent before you choose.
  • If you turn off Settings > Privacy > Gameplay Data Collection, this device stops sending gameplay data from that moment, and a deletion request for the gameplay data already collected is sent at the same time; that data is deleted within 30 days. If you turn it back on, new data is collected from that point. Declining or turning it off does not limit the Game in any way.
  • Party chat (Section 2.B) keeps working even when collection is off. If you do not want party chat information to be sent, do not use multiplayer.
  • Do Not Track: The Game is not a web browser, so it does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals. You can turn collection off with the in-game setting above instead.

5. Disclosure to Third Parties and Sale

  • We do not provide your personal information to third parties, except where the law specifically requires it.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

6. Steam and Data Stored on Your Device

  • Steam lobbies, invitations, connections between players, and the Steam nicknames and avatars shown to other players are processed by Valve Corporation under the Steam Privacy Policy. We do not store this information on any server of our own.
  • In LAN parties, players on the same network exchange IP addresses directly to connect. This information is not sent to us.
  • Save data and settings are stored only on your device.

7. Retention and Deletion

  1. Retention periods are as stated in Section 2. When a retention period ends, the data is deleted automatically and irrecoverably from our service provider's systems.
  2. We have no personal information that other laws require us to keep. We do not download or keep separate copies of raw data.
  3. Deletion requests are reviewed by our privacy officer and passed to our service provider, and the data is deleted from its systems within 30 days.

8. Security

  • We have appointed a privacy officer and limit access to the analytics dashboard to the staff who need it.
  • All data in transit is encrypted, and gameplay data never includes your name, email address, or Steam ID.
  • We do not run our own servers that store personal information; data is stored in the secure facilities of the service provider listed in Section 4.
  • No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is 100% secure. We protect personal information with commercially reasonable means, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The Game is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 (under 13 in the United States). If you are a parent or guardian and learn that your child's information has been collected, please contact us using the details in Section 11, and we will delete it as soon as we confirm it.

10. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

  1. You may ask at any time to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop its processing. For children under 14, these rights are exercised by a legal guardian.
  2. What you can do directly in the Game:
    • Stop processing, withdraw consent, and delete: Turn off Settings > Privacy > Gameplay Data Collection. Collection stops, and the gameplay data already collected is deleted within 30 days.
  3. Requests by email: Contact us using the details in Section 11. We will act without delay and reply within 10 days of receiving your request. Because gameplay data is stored only under a random ID, we may ask you for information needed to locate your records. You can delete your gameplay data right away with the in-game setting in item 2.
  4. You may also make a request through a legal guardian or an authorized agent. In that case, a power of attorney must be submitted.
  5. Requests to access or stop processing may be restricted by law (Articles 35(4) and 37(2) of PIPA).
  6. We verify that the person making a request is you or your authorized agent. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
